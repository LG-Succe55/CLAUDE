— 03 Friction

Capability is here.
The operating model isn't.

Six structural frictions slow the translation of AI capability into business outcome. They are not technology problems — they are organizational ones.

01 / Knowledge

Insight Fragmentation

Customer evidence, qualitative interviews and quantitative signals live in disconnected systems. Synthesis is manual and repeated for every brief.

02 / Brief

Slow Briefing Loop

From request to aligned brief: still measured in days. The bottleneck is not creative — it is decision context and shared interpretation.

03 / Review

Sequential Reviews

Design, product, engineering, legal and brand reviews run as a stack, not a mesh. Each handoff loses fidelity and re-opens settled decisions.

04 / Tooling

Tool Sprawl, No Memory

Fourteen surfaces, zero shared state. Outputs accumulate; institutional memory does not. Every project starts close to zero.

05 / Evidence

No Business Case Loop

Design decisions are framed in qualitative language. The business case is reconstructed late, by other functions, often without design in the room.

06 / Scaling

Talent Bottleneck

The best designers are pulled into firefights. Senior judgement does not scale linearly — and today nothing scales it non-linearly.
