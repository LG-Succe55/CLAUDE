The North Star

Vision & Mission

What future Design organization we are building — and how we move from today to that future.

01Vision

Create a globally connected, AI-enabled Design organization that expands human creativity, accelerates learning and decision-making, preserves Design intelligence, and connects creative intent with trusted product information.

So Stellantis can create more distinctive, feasible, and valuable products — with greater speed and confidence.

02Mission

Transform the highest-value Design workflows by scaling proven AI capabilities, developing targeted workflow intelligence, and progressively connecting Design knowledge, geometry, package, engineering, and cost information into trusted, reusable global capabilities.

The roadmap turns Design ambition into portfolio discipline, value measurement, adoption, and reusable enterprise capability.
