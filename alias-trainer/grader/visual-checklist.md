# Visual checklist (Inspector)

Answer each with yes/no and one line of evidence, from zebra, iso-angle and CV/hull screenshots.

1. Zebra/iso-angle lines flow smoothly across every join, with no kinks or sudden width changes.
2. No unintended ogee (inflection) in any feature line or section comb.
3. Hulls flow "in sympathy": neighbouring hull rows are evenly spaced and similar in shape.
4. CVs are densest where curvature is highest and taper smoothly; no clumps at ends.
5. Every patch is four-sided; corners use a fourth edge rather than a triangle.
6. Feature lines that should look intentional are planar or near-planar.
7. Fillets don't pinch where they turn corners.
8. The fewest spans and lowest degree that hold the shape.
9. Seen from the side and top in orthographic views, the main lines are crisp, not "floppy".

Any "no" is a FAIL even if the numbers pass.
