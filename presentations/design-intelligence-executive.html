02 · How Vehicle Design Works Today
THE TRADITIONAL PROCESS

Five sequential phases. Roughly 58 weeks from first sketch to a model a customer can experience.

Every concept passes through ideation, surfacing, clay, fiberglass, and transport — each phase physical, each phase additive in cost and time.

PHASE 01
Ideation + Selection
6 wks
Designers create loose sketch concepts. Leadership selects directions to develop.
PHASE 02
Concept Surfacing
4 wks
Specialists translate 2D into 3D digital surface. $30K–$60K per concept. Guesswork drives rework.
PHASE 03
Clay Modeling
10+ wks
~1,200 lbs of clay sculpted and refined. $90K materials and labor per concept.
PHASE 04
Fiberglass I/O Model
18 wks
Supplier builds a full-size fiberglass model. $500K–$1M per build.
PHASE 05
Research + Transport
2 wks
Ship to consumer research hubs. $50K–$200K per move, plus risk of damage.
Total program cost per concept exploration
$1.5M – $3M
Cycle time, sketch to market test
~58 weeks

03 · The Accelerated Workflow
AI + AR ACCELERATED WORKFLOW

The same five-phase outcome — delivered in roughly six weeks, digital-first.

AI compresses sketch-to-render. AR replaces physical models with full-size virtual review. Teams stay in exploration longer and commit to physical builds only when truly required.

CURRENT REALITY
58 weeks
$1.5M – $3M per concept exploration
  • Sequential phases, each gating the next
  • Physical clay, fiberglass, transport between sites
  • Interpretation gaps between sketch and surface
  • Limited parallel concept exploration
  • Carbon-intensive: clay heating, milling, shipping
DIGITAL-FIRST WORKFLOW
~6 weeks
Fraction of cost · zero transport · near-zero waste
  • Sketch to photoreal render in seconds (Vizcom, Optic)
  • Full-size theme review in AR (Gravity Sketch)
  • Real-time global collaboration across NA, EU, LATAM, China
  • Multiple themes developed in parallel
  • Physical models built only when strictly necessary

05 · Spatial Design + Review
GRAVITY SKETCH + AR

Designers, leadership, and global studios are reviewing themes at full scale — without a single physical model.

Pass-through AR replaces armature, foam, and fiberglass for early review. Themes are evaluated against benchmarks in the same physical room, on the same day.

FULL-SIZE REVIEW

Walk around a full-size virtual model. Eliminate the cost and lead time of a physical theme model for early decisions.

COMPETITIVE BENCHMARKING

Compare directly against benchmark vehicles in AR — without shipping, without printing, without poster rework.

GLOBAL COLLABORATION

NA, EU, LATAM, and China studios iterate on shared 3D data in real time. Less interpretation drift, fewer miscommunications.

PARALLEL EXPLORATION

Multiple themes developed in parallel in AR. Physical models are built only when truly required to move forward.

10 · Automation
AUTOMATION IN DESIGN OPERATIONS

Remove the friction that pulls designers away from designing.

Most studio time is not spent creating. It is spent reformatting, transferring, validating, and chasing. Automation reclaims that time, predictably and at scale.

WHAT GETS AUTOMATED
  • 01Asset preparation — export pipelines, format conversion, naming, versioning.
  • 02Review preparation — automated deck assembly, comparison views, package overlays.
  • 03Compliance + package checks — early validation against engineering and regulatory constraints.
  • 04Routine documentation — design rationale capture, review minutes, decision logs.
THE PRINCIPLE
A designer's most valuable hour is the one spent in creative judgement.

Automation is not a cost-cutting strategy. It is a creative-capacity strategy.

EXPECTED OUTCOME
More concepts explored. Fewer late surprises. Predictable studio operations.

11 · Agentic AI
AGENTIC AI · CO-CREATORS, NOT REPLACEMENTS

The next leap is intelligent co-creators — partners that work alongside designers in real time.

Agents analyze, suggest, iterate, and handle routine work. The designer stays in authorship. The studio operates with leverage it has never had.

DESIGN ANALYSIS

Agents review large bodies of design data and surface intelligent suggestions on proportion, surfacing, and aesthetic intent.

REAL-TIME 3D ITERATION

Co-creators rapidly build and modify 3D iterations while the designer concentrates on the concept that matters.

DELEGATED WORK

Each designer's agent handles routine handoffs and coordination with other agents, freeing humans for high-judgement work.

AMPLIFIED REACH

Agents multiply what each designer can explore — letting the studio pursue creative directions that were previously out of reach.

Our mission is not to replace designers. Our mission is to empower designers.
